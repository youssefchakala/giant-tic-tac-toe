How to play

  • The board is 9 small tic-tac-toe grids inside one big grid.
  • Get three in a row inside a small grid to win that grid.
  • The square you pick sends your opponent to the matching grid. Play the top-right square of any small grid, and they must play in the top-right grid.
  • If that grid is already won or full, they can play in any open grid.
  • A full small grid with no winner counts for nobody.
  • Win three small grids in a row, across, down or diagonally, to win the game.
  • Online: start a game and send your friend the invite, or type their 4-letter code and tap Join. Whoever started the game keeps it open in their browser. Sides swap on every rematch.
